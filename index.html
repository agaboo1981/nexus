Asked, answered.

Is Nexus a real bank?

No. Nexus is a design concept for a consumer fintech. In a real version, banking services would be provided by regulated partner banks, and Nexus itself would not hold your deposit.

What does a transfer cost?

0.41% of the amount, with a minimum of Â£0.31. The figure is calculated and shown before you confirm, and it does not change afterwards. There is no weekend markup.

Where does the rate come from?

The mid-market rate, the same one you see on a currency board or a financial data feed. Nexus takes no margin on the rate itself; the fee is separate and visible.

How fast does money arrive?

The concept target is seconds on major corridors and a few hours on the rest. The app shows the estimate for your corridor before you send, not after.

Can I open an account today?

Not yet. The sign-up and login flows on this site are part of the design demo. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.

One email when Nexus opens, no spam. This is a design concept, so nothing is actually sent.

Nexus is a design concept for a consumer fintech, not a bank. In a real version, banking services would come from regulated partner banks.
