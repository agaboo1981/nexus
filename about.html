Our mission

Rebuilding finance so it's borderless, instant, and honest.

Nexus started with a simple frustration: moving your own money should not be slow, opaque, or full of hidden fees. So we set out to build a system that works the way money actually should — fast, clear, and on your terms.

Integrity first

Our fee schedule is public and fixed. Every transfer shows exactly what it costs before you confirm it, and security is open to independent audits.

Innovation, in service of users

From automatic insights to instant cross-border settlement, we build features that save you time and money — not features for their own sake.
