Borderless by default.

Nexus exists because moving your own money got slow, priced in percentages nobody explained, and settled at a rate chosen after you paid.

See the plans

Nexus started as a question: why does a transfer take days, cost a slice nobody explained, and arrive with the rate chosen after you paid? The answer is not a faster bank. It is a system that shows you the price first and treats the rate as public knowledge, the same for you as for a treasury desk.

This site is a design concept. The flows work in the browser, the figures are illustrative, and no banking services are offered.

Product facts for the concept, not company metrics.

The price is the price.

The fee is calculated before you confirm and it does not change afterwards. If the mid-market rate is 0.79, that is the rate printed on your receipt. Weekends and small amounts follow the same rule.

Your money is not our inventory.

Balances would sit with regulated partner banks, never on Nexus's own balance sheet. Savings goals are yours to move at any moment, with no lock-ins and no exit fees.

For corridors, not countries.

Most products price by where you live. Nexus prices by the corridor you actually use, so a transfer from London to Lagos costs what it costs, whoever you are.
